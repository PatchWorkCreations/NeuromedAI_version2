Terms of Use

Aira helps you remember what was said. It is not medical advice, a diagnosis, or a replacement for your clinician.

You agree to record only when everyone in the room has consented. You will not use Aira to make medication changes. If something looks urgent, contact your doctor or emergency services.

Recording a visit and getting a plain-language summary is free. Sharing with family and some extra features may be part of Aira Plus later.
