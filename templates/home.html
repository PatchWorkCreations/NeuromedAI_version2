Made to be readable

Accessibility isn't a settings page here.

Most health apps bury readability three menus deep. Aira puts it in the header — and the whole page answers to it, including the summary on the right.

  • Four text sizes that scale the whole layout, not just the body copy — so nothing collapses at large type.
  • Light, dark and true high contrast — the contrast theme is designed, not a filter slapped on top.
  • Easy Read rewrites the page in short sentences for anyone who wants it slower.
  • Keyboard and screen-reader first — 48px targets, visible focus, real landmarks.

Where we stop

What Aira will not do.

Being useful in healthcare means being honest about the edges. Aira sits alongside your clinician — it does not stand in for one, and it will tell you plainly when a question isn't ours to answer.

What happens instead

You get a clear handoff: what to ask, who to ask, and a draft message you can send to your care team in one tap.

Talk to your doctor

  • Replace your doctor

    Aira has no clinical judgement and makes no diagnosis. It helps you carry the conversation, not conduct it.

  • Write the official chart

    Your clinical record stays with your provider. Nothing Aira writes goes into it, and nothing it holds replaces it.

  • Guess about your medication

    Starting, stopping or changing a dose is never an answer Aira gives. You'll always see a route back to a clinician.

  • Record without everyone's consent

    The microphone doesn't open until the room agrees, and it closes the moment anyone asks it to.
